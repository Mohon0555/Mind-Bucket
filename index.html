Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the planner and start. Sign in with Google later if you want your planner on more than one device.

Is it free?

Yes. Mind Bucket is free to use.

What is a bucket?

A regular block of time, like “Slow morning, 8–10 am” or “Full-time job, 2–10 pm”. You fill your buckets with interests, to-dos and ideas, so each one has a time to happen.

Where is my planner saved?

As a guest, in this browser on this device. Once you sign in, in your Google account, where only you can read it.

What goes on the bucket list?

The big things you want to do once, like scuba diving, paragliding or a bungee jump. Give each one a year and a first step, and turn it into a trip plan when you’re ready.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in any phone browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
